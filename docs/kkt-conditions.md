# KKT Conditions

## Problem

For

$$
\min_x \; \tfrac{1}{2} x^T P x + q^T x \quad \text{s.t.} \quad A x = b, \; G x \le h,
$$

with $P \succeq 0$, the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions are necessary and, under convexity, sufficient for optimality.

## Lagrangian

$$
\mathcal{L}(x, \nu, \lambda)
= \tfrac{1}{2} x^T P x + q^T x
+ \nu^T (A x - b)
+ \lambda^T (G x - h).
$$

## KKT System

A triple $(x^\star, \nu^\star, \lambda^\star)$ is optimal if and only if:

1. **Stationarity**

$$
P x^\star + q + A^T \nu^\star + G^T \lambda^\star = 0.
$$

2. **Primal feasibility**

$$
A x^\star = b, \qquad G x^\star \le h.
$$

3. **Dual feasibility**

$$
\lambda^\star \ge 0.
$$

4. **Complementary slackness**

$$
\lambda_i^\star \, (G x^\star - h)_i = 0 \quad \text{for all } i.
$$

## Optimality Interpretation

- Stationarity says the negative gradient of the objective lies in the cone generated by the constraint gradients.
- Dual feasibility says multipliers on inequalities are nonnegative (the sign convention from $\lambda \ge 0$ and $G x - h \le 0$).
- Complementarity says an inequality can be active ($G_i x = h_i$) with positive multiplier, or inactive with zero multiplier; it cannot be both inactive and have a positive multiplier.

For convex QP, the KKT conditions are sufficient: any point satisfying all four is a global minimizer.

## Residual Definitions

For numerical solvers, the KKT conditions are checked via residuals:

$$
r_p \;=\; A x - b, \qquad r_g \;=\; \max(0,\, G x - h),
$$

$$
r_d \;=\; P x + q + A^T \nu + G^T \lambda,
$$

$$
r_c \;=\; \lambda^T (h - G x).
$$

A solver reports convergence when

$$
\|r_p\|_\infty \le \varepsilon_p, \quad
\|r_g\|_\infty \le \varepsilon_p, \quad
\|r_d\|_\infty \le \varepsilon_d, \quad
r_c \le \varepsilon_c.
$$
